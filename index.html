    GlobalSplineFit¶

    Cosmic ray flux parametrizations with uncertainty quantification

    GlobalSplineFit (GSF) is a Python package that provides precise parametrizations of cosmic ray flux and composition based on spline fits to experimental data.

    • GSF Explorer

      Explore the model interactively in your browser — fluxes, composition, uncertainties, data export. No installation needed.

    Citation¶

    Cite the GSF 2025 proceedings until the GSF 2026 paper has an InspireHEP record; this page and the Explorer will switch over together.

    @article{Dembinski:2025nmp,
    author = "Dembinski, Hans and Engel, Ralph Richard and Fedynitch, Anatoli and Fujisue, Kozo",
    title = "{Global Spline Fit GSF-2025 - An update of the data-driven model of the cosmic-ray flux and its mass composition}",
    doi = "10.22323/1.501.0248",
    journal = "PoS",
    volume = "ICRC2025",
    pages = "248",
    year = "2025"
}

    InspireHEP record: Dembinski:2025nmp

    This page and the Explorer both read webapp/citations.json; they switch to the GSF 2026 paper once it has an InspireHEP record.

    Citing a specific parameter set, or one of the models GSF is compared with? The model version table lists the reference for every version, and the solar-modulation potentials carry their own.
